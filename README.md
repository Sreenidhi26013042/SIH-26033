## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is a digital marketplace called Farm Connect, which aims to reduce the number of middlemen between farmers and consumers.

In the current system, agricultural products pass through several intermediaries before reaching consumers. This reduces the profit received by farmers and increases the final price of products.

Farm Connect provides a common platform where farmers can directly list their available crops, quantity and expected price. Buyers such as retailers, supermarkets, hotels and individual consumers can view the products and place orders.

The platform also provides a transportation facility by connecting farmers with available delivery partners. Farmers can track their orders and receive updates about payments.

Another useful feature is a demand prediction system that studies previous orders and market trends to help farmers understand which crops may have higher demand.

The main aim is to make agricultural trading easier, more transparent and beneficial for both farmers and consumers.

## Technical Approach
I have plan to develop Farm Connect as a user-friendly web and mobile application.

Technologies to be used:

Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Backend: Python with Flask.

Database: MySQL.

Maps and location: Google Maps API.

Demand forecasting: Python-based data analysis.

Working Process:

Farmers register and create their profiles.

They upload crop details, quantity, location and expected price.

Buyers browse the available products and place orders.

The farmer receives the order and confirms it.

Transportation is arranged through the platform.

The buyer receives the products and payment is processed.

The system maintains order and sales records for future demand analysis.

The application will be designed with simple navigation so that even farmers with basic smartphone knowledge can use it.

## Feasibility and Viability
Farm Connect is practical because it can be developed using commonly available technologies and smartphones.

The platform can initially be tested with a small group of farmers and local buyers. After successful testing, it can be expanded to nearby villages and markets.

Potential Challenges:

Some farmers may not be familiar with digital applications.

Transportation may not always be available.

Product quality and quantity may vary.

Internet connectivity may be limited in rural areas.

Solutions:

Provide a simple interface with regional language support.

Allow FPOs or local coordinators to help farmers register.

Partner with local transport providers.

Include product details and quality information before order confirmation.

Provide SMS notifications wherever possible.

## Impact and Benefits
Farm Connect can create positive changes for farmers, buyers and consumers.

For Farmers:

Better selling prices.

Direct access to buyers.

Reduced dependence on middlemen.

More opportunities to sell agricultural products.

For Consumers:

Fresh agricultural products.

Reduced additional costs.

Better price transparency.

For Bulk Buyers:

Direct access to farmers and FPOs.

Easy comparison of prices and quantities.

Reliable product sourcing.

For the Environment:

Better route planning.

Reduced unnecessary transportation.

Less food wastage through improved demand planning.

Overall, Farm Connect aims to create a fair and transparent agricultural marketplace.
